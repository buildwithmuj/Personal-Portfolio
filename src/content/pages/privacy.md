---
title: Privacy
description: How this site handles your data. No cookies, no analytics, and a booking calendar that only loads when you open it.
---

This site doesn't set cookies or use analytics, and it doesn't track you.

**Email.** My email address is published so you can contact me. If you email me, I only use your
message to reply.

**Booking a call.** The booking calendar is provided by Cal.com. It only loads when you choose "Book a
30-minute call", and once it has loaded, Cal.com may set its own cookies. When you book, Cal.com
handles your details under [its privacy policy](https://cal.com/privacy).

**Ask me.** Questions you pick or type are matched against my written answers in your browser;
nothing is sent anywhere. If there's no answer yet, the site can open your email app with the
question, and it's only sent if you send it.

**Weather.** The London weather beside the clock comes from
[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), a free weather service. When a page loads, your browser asks
Open-Meteo for the current conditions, which shares your IP address with it, as any web request does.
Nothing else about you is sent. The answer is kept in your browser's session storage for this tab,
reused for up to 15 minutes, and cleared when you close the tab.

**Pause animations.** If you pause the site's animations (the button at the foot of each page), your
browser remembers that choice in its local storage, on your device, until you turn them back on.
It's never sent anywhere.

**Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes standard request data, such as IP
addresses, to serve and protect it, under
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**Questions or removal requests.** Email me at the address on the home page.
