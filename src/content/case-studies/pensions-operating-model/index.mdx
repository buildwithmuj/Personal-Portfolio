## The problem

A pensions administrator wanted to move to automation-driven service delivery, but its processes ran
differently from team to team and weren't written down consistently. Without one shared picture,
automation was hard to plan.

## What I did

- Ran as-is assessments of the core business processes with the teams who carried them out.
- Defined the future-state operating model: who does what, and which steps suit automation.
- Wrote the detailed SOPs and technical documentation, so that handover, training and governance
  worked the same way across onshore and offshore teams.
- Took the findings through with stakeholders to agree what came first.

## The outcome

- One documented, agreed way of working to build automation on.
- Consistent handover and training across teams.
- A clear starting point for automation-driven service delivery.
