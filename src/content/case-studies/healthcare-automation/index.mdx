## The problem

Back-office teams at a UK healthcare group were handling high volumes of work by hand. Turnaround
was slow, mistakes crept in, and nobody had a clear view of which processes were worth automating
first.

## What I did

- Ran workshops with the teams doing the work, to map how each process really ran and where the time
  went.
- Assessed the candidates for automation and built the ROI-focused business cases that won senior
  approval for the roadmap and the investment.
- Designed the solutions with the delivery team: RPA, workflow, document digitisation and process
  orchestration, each where it fitted.
- Coordinated testing, user acceptance testing included, and supported the releases and the first
  weeks after them.
- Wrote the SOPs and documentation the teams needed to run and support what we built.

## The outcome

- Less manual work and faster turnaround times across the back office.
- More consistent processing, with fewer errors to put right.
- An agreed roadmap for what to automate next.
