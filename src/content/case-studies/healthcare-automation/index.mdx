## The problem

Back-office teams at a UK healthcare group were working through high volumes of processing by hand,
and turnaround times suffered for it.

## What I did

I led the automation work. It started with workshops and requirements gathering with the business
and IT teams, to capture how each process ran. From there I assessed which processes were worth
automating, and built the ROI-focused business cases that won senior approval for the roadmap and
the investment.

I designed the solutions with the delivery team, combining RPA and workflow automation with document
digitisation and process orchestration. I then coordinated system and user acceptance testing, and
supported the move into production and the hypercare period after it.

## The outcome

The back office does a lot less by hand now, and turns work around faster on more streamlined
processes.
