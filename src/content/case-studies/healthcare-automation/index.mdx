## The problem

Back-office teams at a UK healthcare group were handling high volumes of work by hand. Turnaround
was slow, mistakes crept in, and nobody had a clear view of which processes were worth automating
first.

## What I did

I started with workshops alongside the teams doing the work, mapping how each process really ran and
where the time went. From there I assessed which processes were worth automating, and built the
ROI-focused business cases that won senior approval for the roadmap and the investment.

I designed the solutions with the delivery team, using RPA, workflow, document digitisation and
process orchestration where each fitted. I then coordinated testing, user acceptance testing
included, supported the releases and the first weeks after them, and wrote the SOPs and
documentation the teams needed to run what we built.

## The outcome

The back office now does less by hand and turns work around faster, with more consistent processing
and fewer errors to put right. The group also has an agreed roadmap for what to automate next.
