## The idea

Listing photos show you one room at a time, and you're left guessing how the place fits together. I
want to see whether AI tools can turn a stay into one seamless 3D tour, so you can walk through it
before you book.

## Why I'm trying it

It's mainly a way to test what AI can do right now. 3D is a long way from the documents and processes
I usually work with, which is part of the appeal.

## Where it is now

An experiment, with no customers behind it. When there's something worth showing, it'll go here.
