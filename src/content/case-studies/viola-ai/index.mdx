## The idea

Research papers are dense, and students often need a quicker way into them. I built Viola AI to
summarise papers and help students work through what they said.

## How I built it

I put it together with Framer, Supabase and Vercel, along with a few other tools, and used OpenAI's
API for the summaries. It wasn't built with AI coding tools: the AI was in the product itself.

## Where it is now

It's archived now. It stayed a concept, built mainly for my own learning, and I never took it out to
users. It was my first go at building a product around generative AI, and a good one to learn from.
