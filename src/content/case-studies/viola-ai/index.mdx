## The idea

Research papers are dense, and students often need a quicker way into them. Viola AI summarised
papers and helped students work through what they said.

## How I built it

I put it together with Framer, Supabase and Vercel, along with a few other tools, and used
OpenAI's API for the summaries. It wasn't built with AI coding tools: the AI was in the product
itself.

## Where it is now

It's archived now. It was my first go at building a product around generative AI, and a good one to
learn from.
