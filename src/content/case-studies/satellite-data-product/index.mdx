## The problem

A global satellite communications company wanted its teams to get insight from their own data,
through self-service dashboards on a cloud data warehouse.

## What I did

As Product Owner I owned the backlog, the priorities and the roadmap for the data-visualisation
dashboards. I worked with data engineers, analysts and business stakeholders to define the data
models, the reporting requirements and the user stories.

I made sure each release was properly tested and that the design could scale, and coordinated the
training that helped people pick the dashboards up.

## The outcome

The business got self-service dashboards on its data warehouse, with the people using them trained
to find their own answers.
