## The problem

Teams at a global satellite communications company relied on analysts for their reports. They needed
to answer their own questions from the data, and sooner than the existing reporting allowed.

## What I did

- Owned the product backlog, the priorities and the roadmap for data-visualisation dashboards on a
  cloud data warehouse.
- Worked with data engineers, analysts and business stakeholders to define the data models, the
  reporting requirements and the user stories.
- Kept each release tested and the design able to scale as more teams came on.
- Ran the training that helped people pick the dashboards up.

## The outcome

- Self-service dashboards the business could use without raising a request.
- Delivered in stages, each release adding to the last.
