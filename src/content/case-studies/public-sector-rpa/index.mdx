## The problem

Teams at a UK public-sector organisation spent a large part of each day on repetitive, rules-based
processing, and there was no agreed way to decide what was worth automating.

## What I did

I ran feasibility assessments of the as-is processes and picked out the strongest candidates for
RPA, then built the cost-benefit analysis and the business case that secured the investment.

I set out the delivery plan and led a phased rollout within the organisation's security, regulatory
and change rules. Alongside that I supported the procurement, through vendor assessments, governance
approvals and sign-off, and ran the training and adoption work with the operational teams.

## The outcome

Staff time moved from manual processing to higher-value work, backed by an approved business case
and a phased rollout. The teams were trained to work with the automations, not around them.
