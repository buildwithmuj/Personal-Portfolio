## The problem

Teams spent a large part of their day on repetitive, rules-based processing.

## What I did

- Assessed processes for automation potential and built the business cases.
- Led delivery of RPA from design through testing and rollout.
- Worked with operational teams to embed the new ways of working.

## The outcome

Staff time moved from manual processing to higher-value work.
