## The problem

Teams at a UK public-sector organisation spent a large part of each day on repetitive, rules-based
processing, and there was no agreed way to decide what was worth automating.

## What I did

- Ran feasibility assessments of the as-is processes and picked out the strongest candidates for
  RPA.
- Built the cost-benefit analysis and the business case that secured the investment.
- Set out the delivery plan and led a phased rollout, within the organisation's security, regulatory
  and change rules.
- Supported the procurement: vendor assessments, governance approvals and sign-off.
- Ran the training and adoption work with the operational teams.

## The outcome

- Staff time moved from manual processing to higher-value work.
- An approved business case, and a phased rollout behind it.
- Teams trained to work with the automations, not around them.
