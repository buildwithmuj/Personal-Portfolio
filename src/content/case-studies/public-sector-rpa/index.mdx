## The problem

A UK public-sector organisation wanted to bring in RPA. It needed to know where automation would pay
off, and a business case strong enough to fund it.

## What I did

I ran feasibility assessments of the as-is processes and picked out the priority candidates for RPA,
then built the cost-benefit analysis and the financial model behind the business case that secured
the investment.

I set out the delivery strategy and roadmap, and coordinated a phased rollout within the
organisation's regulatory, security and change constraints. Alongside that I led the vendor
assessments, supported the procurement, managed the governance approvals and sign-off, and
coordinated the training and adoption work with the teams.

## The outcome

An approved business case, and RPA rolled out in phases across the organisation, with the teams
trained to work with it.
