## The idea

Every automation project I've worked on starts the same way: a few weeks of workshops to find out
how the work really gets done. I want to see how much of that first stretch an AI assistant can take
on.

You'd describe a process in your own words, or hand over the notes and documents you already have.
The assistant asks the questions an analyst would ask, then gives back a map of the process as it
runs today, the steps that cost the most time, and a first view of what is worth automating.

## Why this one

It's the part of my job I know best, and it's where agentic AI looks most useful to me: an assistant
that works through a task step by step, not one that follows a fixed script.

It wouldn't replace sitting with the people who do the work. It would get a team to a decent first
draft in an afternoon, so the workshops can go on the hard questions.

## Where it is now

A concept, not a product. I'm sketching the first version and working out what to test first. If
it's a problem you have too, I'd like to hear about it.
